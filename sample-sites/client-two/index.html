Our Story

A Hondo Institution
Since 2009.

Main Street Grille started as a simple idea: give Hondo a place where neighbors could sit down, eat something real, and feel at home. Fifteen years later, that idea hasn't changed a bit.

Every dish comes out of a scratch kitchen. Steaks cut in-house, sauces made from scratch, and desserts baked fresh daily.
