— Our Story

A Hondo Institution
Since 2009.

Main Street Grille started as a simple idea: give Hondo a place where neighbors could sit down, eat something real, and feel at home. Fifteen years later, that idea hasn't changed a bit.

Every dish we serve comes out of a scratch kitchen. Our steaks are cut in-house, our sauces are made from scratch, and our desserts are baked fresh daily. We don't do shortcuts.
