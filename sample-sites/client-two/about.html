— How It Started

Jim & Linda
Caldwell, Owners.

In 2009, Jim Caldwell quit his oil-field job and Linda left her teaching career to open a restaurant in the building where Jim's father used to run a hardware store. They called it Main Street Grille — because that's exactly what it is.

Everything you eat here comes out of a scratch kitchen. Jim handles the grill and the smoker. Linda runs the front of the house and still bakes every dessert herself. Their daughter Kayla joined the team in 2019 as general manager.

We're not a chain, and we never want to be. We know our regulars by name, and we like it that way.

— How We Do It

The Way We Cook

01

Everything From Scratch

Our sauces, soups, desserts, and breads are made in-house every day. If we can make it ourselves, we do.

02

Texas Raised Ingredients

We source beef from Texas ranches, our peaches come from a farm two counties over, and we buy produce from local growers whenever it's in season.

03

Low and Slow on the Smoker

Our ribs and brisket go on the pit at 5am. By the time lunch rolls around, they've been cooking for six hours. That's not a shortcut — that's the only way.

04

Family Service, Every Time

Every table gets our full attention. Our staff has been with us an average of six years — they're not just employees, they're part of the family.
