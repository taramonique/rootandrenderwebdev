How It Started

Hi, we’re Maria and Carlos.

In 2015, Maria started cleaning homes on weekends to help support our family. What began as a side job quickly grew into something more — because she genuinely cared about every home she walked into.

Carlos joined a year later, and together we built Flores Family Cleaning into one of San Antonio’s most trusted names in residential cleaning. Today our small team of trained, background-checked cleaners serves hundreds of families across the city.

We’re still family-run, still hands-on, and still as committed to quality as the day Maria cleaned that very first house on Elm Street.
