My Story

Hi, I’m Tara.

I moved to Hondo in 2020 after graduating from Texas State University in San Marcos with a Bachelor of Business Administration in Computer Information Systems. I’ve been working in the tech industry ever since — and somewhere along the way I started noticing something that bothered me.

So many incredible local businesses — the kind that are the heartbeat of a community like ours — had no real online presence. No website, no searchable information, just a Facebook page that may or may not be up to date. As a mom and a tech professional, I knew I could do something about that.

Root & Render was born out of a simple desire to help the community I love show up online — without the overwhelm, without the confusion, and without having to figure it all out alone.

My Mission

What Root & Render
Is Really About.

My goal is simple — to give Medina County business owners a way to show up online without the hassle of tech.

I want Root & Render to be the place local businesses turn to when they need a website, social media content, or any kind of digital presence and just don’t have the time or know-how to build it themselves. Whether you’re a restaurant owner, a salon, a towing company, or a boutique — you deserve to be found online, and I’m here to make that happen.

This community has given me a lot. This is my way of giving back.

“Every small town business deserves a website they’re proud to share.”

— Tara, Founder of Root & Render
